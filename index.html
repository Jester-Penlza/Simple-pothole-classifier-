How it works

A compact, explainable pipeline.

  1. 01Resize

    The image becomes a small 16 × 16 working copy.

  2. 02Measure

    Grayscale, edge, and color features are calculated.

  3. 03Standardize

    Features are normalized using the saved training values.

  4. 04Classify

    Logistic regression returns both class probabilities.

Use responsibly

A classroom classifier, not a road-safety decision system.

The model classifies the whole image; it does not locate a pothole, measure its size, or assess road safety. Unusual lighting, shadows, wet pavement, and unfamiliar road surfaces can affect its estimate.
